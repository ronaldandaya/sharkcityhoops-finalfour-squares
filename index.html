How To Play

  1. Tap open squares to select them ($20 each). You can pick multiple squares at once.
  2. Hit "Claim Selected" — enter your name and email so we can send payment info.
  3. Pay via Zelle to sharkcityhoops@gmail.com or wait for an invoice. Payment due by Friday morning.
  4. Once all 100 squares are sold, numbers 0-9 are randomly drawn for the rows and columns — separately for each game.
  5. After each game, check the last digit of both teams' final scores. Where those digits meet is the winning square!
